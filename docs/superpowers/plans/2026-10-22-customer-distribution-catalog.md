# Customer, Distribution, and Catalog Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Add customer management, distribution hierarchy management, admin routes/navigation, and category-to-product filtering.

- Catalog category buttons filter the product grid and count.
- Customer management includes metrics, search/filter, customer list, status actions, and add-customer entry.
- Distribution management includes hierarchy, commission configuration, member counts, status actions, and add-level entry.
- Register `/admin/customers` and `/admin/distribution`.
- Validate tests, typecheck, build, and local HTTP.
